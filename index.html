A private theatre experience where you are the protagonist in a dramatic story.

An evening with Odeum

A private theatre game for six.
You're in the cast.

Think private theatre crossed with a social game like Mafia.

The world, the characters, and the story are written for you. The ending isn't. That's for you and your table to decide.

  1. Arrival

    Meet your group.

    You're matched with other players to form a group of six to enjoy the evening together.

  2. Narrative

    Read your side.

    Every player reads a dramatic story from their own character's point of view.

  3. Game

    Play it out.

    The story advances through structured social games built on trust, betrayal, alliances, and persuasion.

  4. Theatre

    The scene comes to you.

    Actors perform the story's key scenes an arm's length away.

  5. Finale

    Your table writes the ending.

    Reading, playing and watching repeat as the story deepens, until the table makes the choice that decides how it ends.
