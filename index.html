A private theatre experience where you are the protagonist in a dramatic story.

An evening with Odeum

Think of private theatre crossed
with a social game like Mafia.

The story is already waiting for you. You’ll read your character, make choices, and watch the night change around your table.

You don’t need to know how to act. Our actors bring the scenes to life; you decide where the story goes.

  1. 1. Arrival

    Meet your group.

    Come alone or with friends. Each table brings six players together for the evening.

  2. 2. Narrative

    Read your story.

    Each player experiences the story from their own character’s point of view.

  3. 3. Game

    Play it out.

    The story advances through social games built around trust, betrayal, alliances, and persuasion.

  4. 4. Theatre

    The scene comes to you.

    Actors perform the story’s key scenes just an arm’s length away.

  5. 5. Finale

    Your table writes the ending.

    Reading, play, and live performance deepen the story until your table faces the final choice.
