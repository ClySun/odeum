Meet your group.

Come alone or with friends. Each table brings six players together for the evening.

Play it out.

The story advances through social games built around trust, betrayal, alliances, and persuasion.

Summertime in Prague

Czechoslovakia, 1968

You are a writer, and Prague is awakening.

In January 1968, after twenty years of Communist rule, censorship began to loosen in Czechoslovakia. The country welcomed a new spring as Communist Party leader Alexander Dubček announced a program of reform he called “socialism with a human face.”

You are writers for Dialog, an influential literary magazine, pushing the boundaries of what can finally be published. But every article still carries a risk, and it is becoming more critical than ever to tell friend from foe.

Good to know

  • It’s not about acting

    No pressure to perform. You’re simply here to understand your character’s motivations and make the choices they would.

  • A full evening, 6–11 PM

    Four hours of game, plus an hour of setup and debrief. Light snacks and refreshments provided — please come after dinner.

  • Come alone or with friends

    Partners are welcome to sign up together, but the game is also designed for strangers to meet and connect through a shared story.

  • Beta playtest note

    We photograph and film as unobtrusively as possible. By signing up you consent to appearing in future marketing materials. Thank you for helping us make this happen!

Common questions

How do I sign up?

Scroll to Reserve a seat, pick an open character on a date, and send a short request (name, email, phone). We’ll hold the seat as pending and confirm with you by email.

Do I need to sign up with a partner?

No need! While we value partners signing up together so they can enjoy the story as a pair, the game is also designed for strangers to meet and connect through a shared story.

What is your cancellation policy?

We completely understand that things come up. The game requires all six players, so please let us know as soon as possible if you can no longer attend. Five days before the game we send a final confirmation email to double-check that everyone is still available.
