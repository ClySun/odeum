What is Odeum

A private theatre game for six.
You're in the cast.

Odeum seats six people at a table and gives each of them a character to inhabit: a name, a past, and a reason to be in the room. Actors perform around you, drawing you into the world and guiding what happens next. You talk, befriend, scheme, and make choices in character. This isn't an acting challenge; it's a chance to live inside someone else's wants and fears for a few hours.

Think private theatre crossed with a social game like Mafia.

The world, the characters, and the story are written for you. The ending isn't. That's for you and your table to decide.

  • Immersive

    You get your own character, not a seat in a crowd. The room becomes another world for three to four hours.

  • Theatre

    Every story is a full script, written by professional dramatists over months, performed by actors trained to improvise as your choices shape the night.

  • Social

    You arrive as strangers, and by the end you've explored together the deepest wants and desires of the human experience.
