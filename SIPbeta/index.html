The story

You are a writer, and Prague is awakening.

In January 1968, after twenty years of Communist rule, censorship began to loosen in Czechoslovakia. The country welcomed a new spring as Communist Party leader Alexander Dubček announced a program of reform he called “socialism with a human face.”

You are writers for Dialog, an influential literary magazine, pushing the boundaries of what can finally be published. But every article still carries a risk, and it is becoming more critical than ever to tell friend from foe.

Odeum (an Ancient Greek term for a small theatre) seats six players around a table and gives each of them a character to inhabit. Every character has their own desires, secrets, and choices to make. Every character is a protagonist with the power to move the story forward.

Actors perform and guide you through this immersive journey. You will talk, befriend, scheme, and make choices, all while living inside your character’s world.

Good to know

  • It's not about acting

    Please don't feel any pressure to act or perform in a certain way. You are only here to understand your character's motivations and make decisions as they would.

  • A full evening, 6 to 11 PM

    The game lasts four hours, with an additional hour for setup and debrief. Light snacks and refreshments will be provided. Please come after dinner!

  • Signing up with a partner

    Every character in this game has a romantic partner at the table. While you won't be acting out the romance, it is an important part of the story. We recommend bringing your real-life partner and signing up together as a pair.

  • Cross-gender casting

    Ideally, you should feel free to choose whichever character speaks to you. But because romance is an important part of the immersion, cross-gender casting can affect how the pairings work. If you'd like to play a character of another gender, please message me privately and we can figure it out together. If you're signing up with a partner and would both like to swap the genders you play, feel free to try it — though for your first game we recommend staying closer to reality to maximize immersion.

  • Beta game expectations

    We'd love any feedback you have. The most important part will come from observing how you play through the experience, and if you're willing, we'd love a testimonial afterward. We'll be taking photos and videos as nonintrusively as possible during the game; by signing up, you consent to appearing in these marketing materials. Thank you for helping us figure all of this out.

Behind Odeum

Clytze Sun / KLYE-tsee /

The Writer & Host

Clytze is a variation of Clytie, a water nymph in Greek mythology who fell in love with Apollo and was transformed into a sunflower. She chose the name when she was fourteen, not realizing that her last name would one day be anglicized as Sun.

Welcome to her sunny side of the world: a world of heroes and admirable journeys across the sky.
