The story

You are a writer, and Prague is awakening.

In January 1968, after twenty years of Communist rule, censorship began to loosen in Czechoslovakia. The country welcomed a new spring as Communist Party leader Alexander Dubček announced a program of reform he called “socialism with a human face.”

You are writers for Dialog, an influential literary magazine, pushing the boundaries of what can finally be published. But every article still carries a risk, and it is becoming more critical than ever to tell friend from foe.

Odeum (an Ancient Greek term for a small theatre) seats six players around a table and gives each of them a character to inhabit. Every character has their own desires, secrets, and choices to make. Every character is a protagonist with the power to move the story forward.

Actors perform and guide you through this immersive journey. You will talk, befriend, scheme, and make choices, all while living inside your character’s world.

Good to know

  • How the game works

    You read your character booklet chapter by chapter alongside the table. Between chapters, you take part in structured interactions and watch short theatre scenes performed by actors.

  • It's not about acting

    No pressure to perform. You're simply here to understand your character's motivations and make the choices they would.

  • A full evening, 6–11 PM

    Four hours of game, plus an hour of setup and debrief. Light snacks and refreshments provided — please come after dinner!

    We'll photograph and film as unobtrusively as possible. By signing up, you consent to being photographed and filmed and to appearing in future marketing materials. Thank you for helping us make this happen!

Common Questions

Do I need to sign up with a partner?

No need! While we value partners signing up together so they can enjoy the story as a pair, the game is also designed for strangers to meet and connect through a shared story.

What is your cancellation policy?

We completely understand that things come up. However, the game requires all six players, so please let us know as soon as possible if you can no longer attend. Five days before the game, we will send a final confirmation email to double-check that everyone is still available.

For any other questions, feel free to reach out anytime! You can find my contact info in the section below.

Behind Odeum

Clytze Sun / KLYE-tsee /

The Writer & Host

The name, Clytze, is a variation of Clytie, a water nymph in Greek mythology who fell in love with Apollo and was transformed into a sunflower. Clytze chose this name when she was fourteen, not realizing that her last name would one day be anglicized as Sun.

Welcome to her sunny side of the world: a world of heroes and admirable journeys across the sky.
