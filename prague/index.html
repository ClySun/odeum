The story

You are a writer for a literary magazine — and the summer is turning.

In January 1968, Prague experiences its first loosening of censorship after twenty years of Communist rule. As writers for a literary magazine, you are pushing the boundaries of what can be published. But every article carries a risk, and it is becoming critical to tell friend from foe.

Odeum seats six people at a table and gives each of them a character to inhabit — a name, a past, and a reason to be in the room. Actors perform around you; you talk, befriend, scheme, and make choices in character. The ending isn't written. That's for you and your table to decide.

Good to know

  • No acting experience needed

    You'll get a full character dossier in advance. Come as yourself, in your character's shoes.

  • Six players a night

    An intimate cast of strangers. You'll leave having conspired, argued, and confided together.

  • An evening, 6–11 pm

    Four hours of game, plus an hour of setup and debrief. Light refreshments provided.

  • It's a beta

    You're among the very first to play. We'll ask for your honest notes afterwards.
